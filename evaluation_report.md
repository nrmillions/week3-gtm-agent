## Copy quality observations

The 15 PASS results above verify workflow behavior, retries, revision limits and human approval. They do not mean every campaign met the editorial targets. All five final live scenarios still had NEEDS REVISION at the two-cycle limit. The app correctly displayed the remaining issues and required acknowledgement rather than claiming an automatic PASS.

- Entertainment: the final blog had 65 words, below the 80-word checker target.
- Technology: the final blog had 43 words, below the checker target.
- Event: the numeric checker flagged `26`, which may be a date abbreviation; this needs human judgment.
- Ambiguous input: the checker flagged an unsupported `15`; the model can invent timing when the brief is vague.
- Unsupported-claim input: the numeric/phrase tools found no remaining issue, but the reviewer continued to criticize an unsubstantiated claim from the brief. The reviewer can give stale or overly strict feedback.

These observations are taken from the saved final model outputs and critiques. A person must inspect the actual campaign before accepting it. The browser demo is separately generated, so its exact wording and critique can differ.
